# Anti-Patterns

Maloja Plana — Der Schweizer Lebensordner — explicitly rejects the following patterns. These are not future risks to manage; they are design boundaries that must never be crossed.

## Engagement optimization

- No streak counters
- No daily login rewards
- No "you haven't opened the app in X days" notifications
- No completion percentages that create anxiety
- No badges, achievements, or gamification
- No leaderboards or social comparison

## Dopamine UX

- No pull-to-refresh addiction loops
- No infinite scroll
- No notification spam
- No red badge counts on navigation
- No urgency colors (flashing red) for incomplete sections
- No countdown timers for non-critical actions

## Shame mechanics

- No "you failed your budget" messaging
- No negative framing of incomplete data
- No guilt-driven reminders
- No comparison to "average" users
- No punitive language for missed deadlines
- Empty states should feel inviting, not accusatory

## Growth hacking

- No dark patterns in onboarding
- No forced account creation
- No "invite friends" mechanics
- No data harvesting disguised as features
- No A/B testing that degrades user experience
- No artificial scarcity or urgency

## Financial pressure

- No aggressive savings targets
- No investment advice or nudges
- No "you should be saving X%" judgments
- No finance-bro culture or hustle aesthetics
- No strict budgeting enforcement
- Budget guidance is orientation, not surveillance

## Data exploitation

- No analytics or telemetry
- No user behavior tracking
- No selling or sharing of data
- No advertising
- No profiling
- No third-party data integrations without explicit user action

## Accessibility exclusion

- No features that require specific devices
- No flows that assume financial literacy
- No language that assumes legal knowledge
- No UX that assumes neurotypical cognitive patterns
- No assumptions about family structure, gender, religion, or origin
- No interfaces that require sustained attention or rapid decision-making

## Trust erosion

- No hidden data transfers
- No pre-checked consent boxes
- No Terms of Service that contradict the privacy promise
- No "we updated our policy" dark patterns
- No features that silently change behavior
- No auto-submission of any data to authorities or third parties
