# Maloja Plana — Alpha Testing Guide

Welcome to the closed alpha of Maloja Plana, a privacy-first Swiss life organizer.

## What is Maloja Plana?

Maloja Plana helps immigrants, refugees, and expats in Switzerland organize their personal documents, finances, insurance, and official matters — in one calm, private app.

**Everything stays on your device.** There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking, and no analytics. Your data never leaves your browser.

## How to access

Open the link provided to you in a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge).

1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge

## How to test

Please use the app naturally for 1-2 weeks. Try the things you would actually need in your daily life.

### Suggested test areas

- **Personal data**: Fill in your name, address, phone, AHV number
- **Documents**: Upload a document photo, set an expiry date
- **Finances**: Enter your income and expenses
- **Insurance**: Enter your health insurance details
- **Calendar**: Create a reminder for a deadline
- **Export**: Try exporting your data as JSON
- **Encrypted backup**: Try creating a password-protected backup
- **Import**: Export, then import the file again — does it restore correctly?
- **Language**: Switch between EN, DE, FR, IT — is the translation natural?
- **Dark/Light mode**: Toggle the theme — is everything readable?
- **Mobile**: Use the app on your phone — does everything fit?

### What to pay attention to

- Does anything feel confusing or unclear?
- Is any text hard to understand?
- Does the app feel calm or stressful?
- Would you trust this app with real personal data?
- Would you store real documents here?
- What life area is missing?
- Did anything break or look wrong?

## How to give feedback

Please share your feedback using one of these methods:

1. **Written notes** — write down anything you notice while testing
2. **Screenshot** — take a screenshot if something looks wrong
3. **Voice message** — record a quick voice note describing the issue

Send your feedback to the contact provided when you received the test link.

## Important notes

- **Your data is only stored in your browser.** If you clear browser data, it is gone.
- **Export regularly.** Use the backup feature to save your data to a file.
- **This is alpha software.** Some features may change. Please report bugs.
- **No data is sent anywhere.** We cannot see your data and cannot recover it if lost.

## Privacy promise

- No accounts, no login, no registration
- No analytics, no tracking, no cookies (except your own saved data)
- No data leaves your device
- No network requests except loading the app itself
- Encrypted backup uses AES-256, processed entirely on your device
- Your passphrase is never transmitted or stored

Thank you for helping us build something that matters.
