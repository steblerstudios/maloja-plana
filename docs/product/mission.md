# Maloja Plana

## The Swiss Life Organizer

Maloja Plana is a calm-tech system for organizing real life with clarity, trust and reduced mental overload.

The goal is not productivity maximization.

The goal is:
- orientation
- emotional calm
- trustworthy organization
- administrative clarity
- reduced cognitive burden
- human-readable life systems

Maloja Plana is designed especially for people experiencing:
- administrative overwhelm
- neurodivergence
- burnout
- fragmented documents
- deadline stress
- bureaucratic complexity
- emotional overload

The system follows:
- calm technology principles
- privacy-first architecture
- local-first philosophy
- emotionally safe UX
- Swiss trust-oriented design
