# Life Events

## Purpose

Life events are moments where multiple systems change at once.

Maloja Plana should help users understand:
- what changes
- what may require action
- what documents are involved
- which domains are connected

The goal is orientation, not automation.

---

# Common Life Events

## Moving / Change of Residence

Possible impacts:
- municipality
- canton
- taxes
- insurance premiums
- parking permits
- voting registration
- school systems
- social support eligibility

Possible related documents:
- rental contract
- registration confirmation
- insurance updates

Connected domains:
- housing
- finances
- mobility
- legal obligations

---

# Design Principles

Life-event guidance should:
- reduce confusion
- avoid fear-based UX
- explain relationships clearly
- support gradual progress
- never pressure users

The system should help users see:
"what is connected to what."

Not:
"You forgot something."

---

# Important Principle

Life events are human events first.

Administrative systems are secondary.
