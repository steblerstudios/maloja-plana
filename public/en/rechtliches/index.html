Data protection on these pages

The explanatory pages are static HTML. They load no scripts, no cookies and no third-party resources; the fonts are on the same server. The home page also loads small scripts of its own from the same server: one redirects old app addresses to /app/, one animates the images of the journey. Anyone who used the app at the main address before receives a third one, once, on their next visit to one of these pages: it empties the app’s old cache in the browser, unregisters itself and reloads the page once; it does not touch your data on the device. These scripts set no cookies and send no data. There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising.

When the pages are accessed, technical data nevertheless arises at the host — Infomaniak Network SA, Rue Eugène-Marziano 25, 1227 Les Acacias / Geneva, with data centres in Switzerland: IP address in the server logs, browser type, operating system and time of access. This is technically necessary for delivery and operational security. Infomaniak acts as a processor in doing so (Art. 9 nDSG). According to Infomaniak’s own information, access and error logs are kept for at least 7 days (Infomaniak support FAQ 1926, retrieved on 23 September 2026).

There are no further recipients: no analytics services, no social media embeds, no passing on and no sale of data.

Contact by email

If you write to info@malojaplana.ch, we process your address, the content of your message and any attachments in order to reply. The mailbox is hosted by Infomaniak in Switzerland. The correspondence is kept for as long as the enquiry and the usual follow-up questions require, then deleted. It is not passed on to third parties unless required by law.

Waiting list: If you write to us to join the waiting list, we keep your message and your email address for that purpose only. We will write to you at most twice: when the beta ends and, if it happens, when Maloja Plana appears in the App Store. After that we delete the message and the address, at the latest on 31 December 2027, even if there is no App Store version by then. On request we delete them without delay; a reply saying “unsubscribe” (or “abmelden”) is enough. We do not use the address for anything else, including a newsletter.

Data in the application

What you enter in Maloja Plana stays on your device (localStorage, IndexedDB and, for shared files, the browser cache). There is no account, no login and no transmission to the provider or to third parties. Anyone wanting to delete the data deletes it on the device — there is no second copy anywhere else.

Particularly sensitive information (health, social assistance, beliefs) is likewise stored only locally by the app. The automatic backup copies in the browser are not encrypted.

Because nothing is transmitted, the provider also cannot give information about your entries: it has never seen them. The detailed version with all rights under the Swiss Data Protection Act is in the app under “Privacy & Legal”.
