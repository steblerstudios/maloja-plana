# Reality Walkthrough Notes

## Purpose

These notes capture how Maloja Plana feels when used like a real person under real-life pressure.

This is not a technical audit.

It is a calm UX and trust audit.

---

## Test Personas

### Overwhelmed Single Person
Questions:
- Is it clear where to start?
- Are there too many fields at once?
- Does the dashboard feel calming?
- Are missing fields stressful?

### Older Person
Questions:
- Is the text large enough?
- Are buttons obvious?
- Are technical terms explained?
- Is navigation predictable?

### Person With Limited German
Questions:
- Are sentences short?
- Are icons helpful?
- Are Swiss terms understandable?
- Is language switching easy?

### Recently Unemployed Person
Questions:
- Does the app avoid shame?
- Are RAV-related workflows understandable?
- Are financial sections calm?
- Are reminders helpful without pressure?

### Person Near Retirement
Questions:
- Are AHV, BVG and EL concepts visible enough?
- Is pension planning too hidden?
- Are important documents easy to find?

### Person With ADHD / Burnout
Questions:
- Is the visual hierarchy calm?
- Are there too many choices?
- Are forms overwhelming?
- Are next steps obvious?

## Notes

### Dashboard
- What feels calm?
- What feels crowded?
- What should be shown first?
- What should be hidden?

### Chapters
- Which chapters feel too form-heavy?
- Where is intro text needed?
- Which fields need explanation?
- Which fields feel sensitive?

### Documents
- Is upload understandable?
- Are expiry dates clear?
- Are categories obvious?
- Does document storage feel trustworthy?

### Budget
- Does the budget feel supportive?
- Does anything feel shame-based?
- Are reference values clear?
- Are household realities represented?

### Calendar / Reminders
- Are reminders easy to add?
- Are recurring reminders understandable?
- Are health reminders clearly informational only?
- Are overdue states calm?

### Swiss Support Logic
- Are canton differences explained?
- Are Sozialhilfe and IPV shown carefully?
- Is uncertainty communicated clearly?
